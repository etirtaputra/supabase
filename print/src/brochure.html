01Which UPS do I need?
Line-interactiveFor computers, cash registers and small office equipment.
OnlineFor servers, medical devices and machines. The output is always clean, with zero transfer time.
ModularFor data centers that will grow. Add modules when your load increases.

Tell us your load in watts and how long you need backup power. Our engineers size the system for you.

02On-grid, off-grid or hybrid?
On-gridConnects to PLN. Your panels supply power in the day, so your bill goes down. It has no battery, so it stops during a blackout.
Off-gridHas no PLN connection. Batteries store energy for the night. Use it for villages, islands and telecom towers.
HybridConnects to PLN and has batteries. It lowers your bill and keeps the power on during a blackout.

On-grid solar usually pays for itself in 6 to 10 years. The exact time depends on your electricity bill, your roof and when you use power.
